A completely free, offline‑first loyalty engine that lives in your customers' Apple Wallet. No fees, no accounts, no monthly bills, and no internet required after initial setup.

Start Building Free How it works

Built to Keep Customers Coming Back

No accounts or no monthly fees. Set your own rewards, automate gentle reminders, and turn every visit into a lasting relationship.

The Customer Experience

  • Personal Wallet Card: Display your store name proudly as a native card in the user's Wallet.
  • Custom Stamps: Let your brand shine. Select the exact stamp style you want to show in the app.
  • Win Back Inactives: Automated, gentle local notifications remind customers to visit if they've been away too long.

Total Store Control

  • You Set the Rules: Total freedom to define your own rewards (e.g., "10th Coffee Free"). We enforce nothing.
  • 3 Security Modes: Choose between Basic, Simple, or Secure modes to match your store's pace and fraud-prevention needs.
  • Location Perks: Geofencing triggers nearby alerts or bonus stamps during happy hour to drive foot traffic.

Effortless Operations

  • Batch Printing: Print a single static QR code, or generate the next 7 days' worth of rotating codes for higher security.
  • Instant Backup & Restore: Download your store's configuration file. If you ever switch devices or clear your browser, just upload it to restore everything instantly.
  • Zero Hardware Required: Run everything from your existing phone, tablet, or POS web browser.
